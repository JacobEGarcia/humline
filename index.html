Humsuite · 02 · Voice as control

HUMLINE

Your voice is the line. Hum higher to climb, lower to sink, and thread the gates. Stop humming and you fall. How far can one breath take you?

DISTANCE 0 mBEST 0 mNOTE --
Allow the microphone. Hum a comfortable middle note to hold altitude.

Pitch is tracked on your device and never uploaded. Gates drift with your range: after ten seconds the game learns where your voice lives and builds the course around it. Inspired by the hum-driven music tools from The Fixed Seed Company.
